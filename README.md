# Beryl

A personal finance app for Android with a liquid glass design. It's still in development: these are the test builds,
before it reaches Google Play.

✅ Free · No ads · No account · Your data stays on your phone

🎬 [Watch the 37-second video](https://youtube.com/shorts/af8XzG-98WA)

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](../../releases/latest) on your Android phone.
2. Under **Assets**, tap `beryl-x.y.z.apk` to download it.
3. Open the file. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser: that's normal for apps outside the
   Play Store.

📱 Requires Android 7.0 or newer.

**Updates:** install the new APK over the old one; your data stays. To get notified automatically, add this repository
to [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium).

⚠️ **This is a test version.** When Beryl reaches Google Play you'll need to uninstall this one first, and since your
data only lives on your phone, it won't carry over.

## What it does

- Log an expense in a few seconds; it suggests the category as you type
- **Free Money**: what you can spend without touching what you owe or the bills on the way
- A daily summary of how your day went
- Budgets per category that follow your payday (or the calendar month)
- Search across all your transactions
- Three visual styles: Liquid glass, Satin and Flat, in light or dark
- English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian
